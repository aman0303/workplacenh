Frequently Asked Questions

What load capacities are available for assembly line workstations?

Workplace Modular Systems offers workstations ranging from 500 lbs per station up to 1,500 lbs evenly distributed. In-line and corner configurations with three leg sections can support up to 750 lbs. The Heavy Duty Workstation tops out at 1,500 lbs, making it suitable for the most demanding aluminum extrusion assembly operations.

Can workstations be configured for in-line assembly line layouts?

Yes. In-line configurations are available across Standard, Heavy Duty, and Height Adjustable Workstation product lines. These gap-free connected worksurfaces are ideal for cable assembly, long-part handling, and continuous assembly line workflows, supporting multiple workers along a single uninterrupted bench run.

Are height-adjustable options available for assembly line workstations?

Yes. Workstations are available with motorized Direct Drive height adjustment (14 inches of travel), manual adjustment ranging from 30" to 42" depending on model, or fixed leg leveler setups. Motorized options allow effortless sit-stand transitions at the touch of a button, which is beneficial for multi-shift assembly operations.

What worksurface materials are available for assembly applications?

Available worksurface materials include plastic laminate, steel, maple butcher block, phenolic resin, epoxy resin, and ESD-safe laminate. For aluminum extrusion assembly environments, steel and laminate tops offer durability against heavy parts and tooling, while ESD-safe surfaces are available for electronics-sensitive assembly tasks.

How quickly can custom assembly line workstations be delivered?

Standard custom orders are generally delivered within 30–45 calendar days depending on configuration complexity. Quick Ship products for popular configurations ship in under 14 days. Domestic manufacturing in Londonderry, New Hampshire eliminates offshore lead-time risk and tariff exposure, ensuring predictable project timelines.

Can existing stationary workstations be converted to mobile units?

Yes. The Mobile Retrofit Kit available for the Heavy Duty Workstation line allows existing stationary units to be converted into mobile configurations. Wide-range caster options are also available across product lines, providing the flexibility to reposition workstations as production layouts evolve.

What accessories and storage options can be added to assembly workstations?

Workplace Modular Systems offers over 1,000 configurable options including storage cabinets, drawers, shelves, overhead power strips (15A or 20A), vertical utility chases for power and data, caster sets, and specialty accessories. The fully modular design allows accessories to be added, removed, or reconfigured at any time without replacing the entire workstation.
