Frequently Asked Questions

What is the load capacity of Workplace Modular Systems' mass spectrometry workstations?

All mass spectrometry and chromatography workstations from Workplace Modular Systems are rated to 1,000 lbs. This capacity is engineered to support heavy analytical instruments including LC-MS, GC-MS, and ICP-MS systems along with their ancillary equipment such as vacuum pumps, autosamplers, and detector stacks.

Which OEM instrument brands are compatible with these lab workstations?

The workstations are compatible with all major analytical instrument manufacturers including Waters, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Agilent Technologies, Beckman Coulter, Bruker, PerkinElmer, Shimadzu, Sciex, and Siemens Diagnostics. Workplace Modular Systems holds named OEM partner status with Waters, Siemens Diagnostics, and Beckman Coulter.

Are the lab benches compliant with chemical resistance and laboratory safety standards?

Yes. All workstations comply with SEFA 8 chemical resistance standards for laboratory furniture surfaces. They are also ISO/IEC 17025 compatible for analytical lab environments and BSL-1 Ready, with BSL 2-3 configurations currently in development. Designs are built to support OSHA 1910 lab safety requirements.

How many configuration options are available, and can workstations be customized?

There are 9 standard sizes and configuration options available. Beyond these, fully custom configurations can be designed to accommodate specific OEM instrument footprints, including ICP-MS torch boxes, LC-MS autosamplers, and GC-MS detector stacks. Custom tubing cutouts, vacuum pump enclosures, and solvent storage can all be integrated.

What are the lead times for delivery?

Quick Ship products for popular configurations ship in fewer than 14 days. Standard custom orders are manufactured and delivered within 30–45 calendar days depending on complexity. All products are manufactured domestically in Londonderry, NH, eliminating offshore supply chain delays.

Do these workstations support height adjustment for ergonomic use?

The Research & Analytical Lab Workstation series supports manual height adjustment from 30" to 37", allowing technicians to configure bench height for ergonomic comfort during sample analysis and method development workflows. Other models in the line offer fixed-height configurations with leveler options.

What work surface materials are available for these lab benches?

Work surfaces are available in Epoxy Resin, Phenolic Resin, Post-formed Plastic Laminate, and Stainless Steel, depending on the product series. Special-length work surfaces can also be fabricated. Epoxy and Phenolic Resin surfaces provide the highest chemical resistance, making them ideal for solvent-intensive mass spectrometry workflows.
